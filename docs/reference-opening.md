The listening comparison contains eight variations of `08b0195e` and eight independent Long performances at 121 Hz and 44.1 kHz stereo. The same sixteen seeds are retained. No quality, pace or timbre threshold was used to accept or reject them.

The related variations inherit the 28 melodic pitches selected from the original `08b0195e` g3 recording through source time 2:00. The source time identifies which notes to inherit; it does not fix their timestamps in a new performance. Twenty-seven pitches remain unchanged. Strike 23 is lowered one semitone to replace the original tritone leap with a perfect fourth.

Each child seed sets the tempo from its first strike, along with the opening bell recipes, panning and room realization. The accompaniment retains its source pitches and relative entry, with duration scaled to the child tempo and newly seeded detuning and panning. The approved selective FM refinement is applied after the inherited pitches are assigned. The mirrored continuation begins on the next seeded beat, with natural bell tails overlapping. Both directions of each mirrored phrase retain matching intervals.

The new complete recording lasts 1313.742 seconds. The Long planner chooses complete mirrored passes nearest the seeded target while keeping the entire recording within 10–30 minutes. It permits four or more body passes so slow seeds can accommodate the longer inherited opening.
