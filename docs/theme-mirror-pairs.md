# Complete themes and immediate mirrors

A Long section states the complete original theme, then plays every strike in reverse order before the organ interlude. The final forward note is struck again on the next beat to begin the return. Both occurrences use identical pitch, carrier offsets, FM ratios, initial and terminal indices, amplitudes, duration and planned panning. Inter-onset gaps are reflected around that turning interval; the child seed controls the nominal tempo throughout. Natural attacks and decays run forward.

The next section repeats the same pitches with a fresh set of strike recipes, then mirrors those recipes. Palettes progress independently between sections rather than retracing a whole-piece palette sequence. Organ interludes retain their expanding/contracting arch, contextual harmony, filtered Principal sound, wide slow spectral chorus and reverb. They occur only after complete theme–mirror pairs, and the final pair ends with its full tail.

The source selection inherits pitches only. Each child seed supplies its tempo, strike recipes and room. Consequently, the 28-note phrase selected through source time 2:00 occupies a different elapsed duration in each new recording. The phrase becomes the recurring theme in every pair, rather than a one-time prefix.

Each pair has 56 strikes for `g36`, or 22 for `g35` and `g37`. At least two complete pairs must fit within 10–30 minutes, including interludes and tails. A reference theme that cannot fit at a seed’s tempo reports an explicit error. Batch selection may reject such a seed for duration fit only; it does not use a musical-quality score.

The approved E FM rule is applied after pitch assignment. This preserves exact corresponding forward/reverse recipes. New Short performances and complete earlier replay records are unchanged, including `0393ded7` g3, g33-b32 and g33-b34. The public interface has no new controls. Older derived recordings remain available through their complete filenames and replay URLs.
